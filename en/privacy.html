1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following information provides a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that can be used to identify you personally. Detailed information on data protection can be found in our privacy policy below.

Data collection on this website

Who is responsible for data collection on this website?

Data processing on this website is carried out by the website operator. You can find the operator’s contact details in the legal notice of this website.

How do we collect your data?

On the one hand, your data is collected when you provide it to us. This may, for example, be data that you send us by email.

Other data is collected automatically by our IT systems and the services we use when you visit the website. This is mainly technical data (e.g. web browser, operating system or time of page access). This data is collected automatically as soon as you enter this website.

What do we use your data for?

Part of the data is collected to ensure that the website is provided without errors. Other data is used for statistical analysis of how the website is used. This analysis is carried out without cookies and without profiling (see section 5, Umami).

What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to receive information free of charge about the origin, recipients and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have the right to request the correction or deletion of this data. If you have given consent to data processing, you can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right, under certain circumstances, to request the restriction of the processing of your personal data. Furthermore, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

You can contact us at any time at the address given in the legal notice regarding this and any other questions on data protection.

4. Data collection on this website

Server log files

The host of this website (GitHub) automatically collects and stores information in so-called server log files, which your browser transmits automatically. These are:

  • browser type and browser version
  • operating system used
  • referrer URL
  • host name of the accessing computer
  • time of the server request
  • IP address

This data is not merged with other data sources. This data is collected on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The website operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimisation of the website. For this purpose, the server log files must be recorded. We ourselves have no access to these log files. The storage period is determined by GitHub.

Enquiries by email

If you send us enquiries by email, your details, including the contact details you provide, will be stored by us for the purpose of processing the enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass on this data without your consent. Your emails are processed by our email provider Namecheap, Inc., Phoenix, Arizona, USA. As the provider is based in the USA, data may be transferred to a third country. This transfer is based on the safeguards stated by the provider (standard contractual clauses).

This data is processed on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your enquiry is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary for the implementation of pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, the processing is based on our legitimate interest in the effective handling of enquiries addressed to us (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) or on your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), if this has been requested.

Contact form (Tally)

You can use our contact form for enquiries. It is provided via the Tally service of Tally BV, Ghent, Belgium. The information you enter in the form (name, email address, message) is processed to handle your enquiry and stored on servers in the European Union. A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is in place with Tally.

The processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your enquiry is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary for the implementation of pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, it is based on our legitimate interest in the effective handling of enquiries addressed to us (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data is deleted once your enquiry has been fully processed, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Further information can be found in Tally’s privacy policy at: https://tally.so/privacy.

5. Plugins and tools

Fonts (hosted locally)

For uniform display, this website uses fonts that are hosted locally on our server. When you access the website, no connections are established to third-party servers (e.g. Google Fonts); no data is transmitted to third parties as a result.

Umami Analytics (Umami Cloud, data region European Union)

This website uses Umami, a web analytics service that works without cookies. We use the cloud version of Umami Software, Inc., 28 Geary St, Suite 650 #243, San Francisco, California, USA, with the data region European Union. Umami helps us understand which content is visited so that we can improve the website.

What data is processed? When a page is accessed, a script is loaded from cloud.umami.is. Your IP address and technical information about your browser are transmitted to Umami. The page accessed, the referring page (referrer), browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and the approximate country of origin are analysed. In addition, individual interactions are counted anonymously, such as clicking an enquiry button, selecting a tab under “For your institution”, starting the video, downloading the information sheet, copying the email address or reaching a section of the page. These events contain no information about you as a person. According to Umami, your IP address is not stored. No cookies are set and no information is stored on your device. Data is not combined with other data, and there is no tracking across websites.

Legal basis. The processing is based on our legitimate interest in the statistical analysis of the use of our offering (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can object to the processing at any time (Art. 21 GDPR), for example by email to the address given in the legal notice. If the “Do Not Track” setting is activated in your browser, your visit will not be counted.

Data processing and third country. A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is in place with Umami, which forms part of Umami’s terms of use: https://umami.is/dpa. The data is stored in the European Union. As the provider is based in the USA, access from a third country cannot be completely ruled out; it is safeguarded by standard contractual clauses.

Storage period. The statistical data is only stored for as long as is necessary for the analysis and is then deleted. Further information can be found in Umami’s privacy policy at: https://umami.is/privacy.

YouTube (two-click embedding)

This website embeds a video via the YouTube service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The embedding uses the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and is only loaded when you actively click on the play area. Merely visiting the website does not transmit any data to YouTube.

A connection to YouTube’s servers is only established once you start the video. The YouTube server is informed which of our pages you have visited; YouTube may also process further data (e.g. IP address) and may use cookies or similar technologies. If you are logged into your YouTube account, you enable YouTube to assign your browsing behaviour directly to your personal profile. You can prevent this by logging out of your YouTube account beforehand.

The video is only loaded once you give your consent by clicking on the play area. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by no longer playing the video and deleting the cookies set by YouTube in your browser. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA. This transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Further information on the handling of user data can be found in YouTube’s privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en.
